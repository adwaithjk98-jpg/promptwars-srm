# Problem Statement: FairShift, Decent Work Platform

Prompt Wars 2026 · ACM Student Chapter, SRMIST · Theme: UN SDG 8, Decent Work and Economic Growth

## What to build
A platform that makes workplaces fairer, safer, and more transparent for employee-intensive organisations. It gives workers clarity and a voice, and gives employers actionable insight into workforce health.

## Context
Large workforces are still managed with paper registers, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp groups:
- **Construction:** daily shift workers on active sites
- **Colleges and schools:** campus support and facility staff
- **Hospitals:** round-the-clock care and sanitation teams
- **Factories:** high-volume floor and line operators

### Worker pain points
- **Opaque pay calculations:** unclear salary breakdowns; overtime hours not counted
- **Fear of identification:** workers hesitate to raise complaints or give honest feedback
- **Unreported safety risks:** hazards ignored until someone is injured
- **Lost career history:** skills aren't recorded, so workers restart from zero when changing jobs

### Employer blind spots
- **Zero workforce visibility:** no clear view of who is overworked or unhappy
- **High attrition risk:** can't spot employees likely to quit early
- **Delayed escalation:** issues reach management only after they become severe

### Accessibility constraints
- **Regional languages:** workers mainly communicate in their native language
- **Limited literacy:** interfaces must support voice and visual cues
- **Basic smartphones:** must run smoothly on low-end devices

### Cross-industry requirement
Must work across **at least 2 distinct industries** (for example, a construction site and a college).

## Example features (suggestions; custom ideas welcome)
- **Shift Tracking:** QR or location check-in, with automatic overtime calculation
- **Clear Payslips:** a simple salary breakdown in the worker's native language
- **Safety Reporting:** voice or text hazard reports with status tracking
- **Honest Feedback:** anonymous reviews of managers and conditions
- **Skill Passport:** a portable record of training and experience
- **Fatigue Alerts:** flags excessive hours or back-to-back shifts
- **Admin Dashboard:** attendance trends, complaints, quit risks
- **Voice and Local:** multilingual voice support on low-end phones

## Deliverables
1. **3 or more working features** (a functional prototype)
2. **2 UIs:** one for workers, one for employers/admins
3. **2 or more industry use cases:** a short explanation of adoption across sectors

## AI evaluator (scores submissions out of 100)
1. **Code quality:** clean, readable, well-organised, meaningful names
2. **Security:** zero exposed keys or credentials
3. **Efficiency:** sensible resource use without bloat; should handle 100–1,000 users
4. **Testing:** features verified with unit tests
5. **Accessibility:** readable contrast, keyboard navigation
6. **Problem alignment:** directly solves this challenge statement
