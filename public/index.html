GL Groundline

Decent work platform · UN SDG 8

Fair pay you can see.
A voice you can trust.

Groundline replaces paper registers and WhatsApp groups for construction sites, hospitals and other large workforces. Workers see exactly how their pay is calculated and can report problems safely. Managers see who is overworked before it becomes a crisis.

  • Transparent pay. One tap to check in; hours, overtime and allowances are calculated automatically.
  • Safe reporting. Report hazards or unfair treatment, anonymously if needed, and track what happens next.
  • Early warnings. Fatigue alerts and quit-risk signals reach managers before problems escalate.
  • Built for every worker. English, Tamil and Hindi, with read-aloud for low literacy and basic phones.
